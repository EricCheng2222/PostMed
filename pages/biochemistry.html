Biochemistry

Half of the 高醫 後醫 science paper is biochemistry — 45 questions, 75 marks, sat in English alongside the general-biology half. KMU cites Lehninger when it defends a disputed answer, so this is a real biochemistry standard, not a survey 概論. And the weighting is not what most study plans assume: metabolism is about 40% of it, and lipids alone outweigh every other single topic.

10 notes Weighted by 7 years of past papers 100 self-test questions KMU Post-Bacc Medicine · Informatics Track

What the paper actually asks

The ideaBefore you read a single note, know where the marks are. Almost everyone revising this paper over-studies the citric acid cycle and under-studies lipids — and the past papers say that is exactly backwards. The TCA cycle is worth about one question a year on its own; lipid metabolism is worth nearly six, and rising.

The format

One paper, 「普通生物及生化概論」, 150 of 400 written marks. 90 single-choice questions with five options, 100 minutes, entirely in English. Q16–30 and Q61–90 are the biochemistry half. About 66 seconds per question.

The marking

Wrong answers are penalised: −0.25 in Part I, −0.5 in Part II, floored at zero per section. With five options a blind guess is exactly break-even — so once you eliminate one option, answering beats leaving it blank. Never abandon a question you have already thought about.

The source

KMU's official answer-dispute documents cite Lehninger Principles of Biochemistry, 8th ed repeatedly, down to specific figures. Figure-level detail is fair game, and these notes are pitched at that depth.

Two traps in how people revise this

The TCA cycle is light. About 3% standalone — roughly one question a year. It matters, but mostly embedded in oxidative phosphorylation, anaplerosis and carbon-labelling questions. Lipids are heavy. 12.7%, averaging almost six questions a paper and trending up — and they are the block most study plans shortchange.

Where the paper is moving

The 115 paper pivoted hard: signalling and clinical pharmacology took 14 of the 45 biochemistry questions, against 0–3 in every earlier year — GPCRs, RTKs, Ras, NO→cGMP, statins, tamoxifen, GLP-1 agonists. Meanwhile molecular biology shrank to six, with no replication or repair question at all. Notes 09 and 10 target that shift.

Bioenergetics, the TCA cycle and oxidative phosphorylation

The counter-intuitive one: oxidative phosphorylation outweighs the TCA cycle more than two to one, and is tested mechanistically.
