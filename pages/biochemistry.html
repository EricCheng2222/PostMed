Biochemistry

Biochemistry rewards anyone who fixes the energy scale first. Once you know what a bond is worth in kJ/mol and how much thermal energy is available at 37 °C, protein folding, membrane self-assembly and denaturation stop being lists to memorise and become consequences you can derive.
